## Research context

During my M.S. in Computational Biology at Carnegie Mellon University, I worked in the Pfenning lab studying evolutionary patterns in mammals through enhancer activity.

Vocal-learning TACIT explores the application of machine learning methods to an evolutionary trait: vocal learning.

## Research focus

- Machine learning applied to biological traits
- Enhancer activity across mammals
- Evolutionary patterns and vocal learning
